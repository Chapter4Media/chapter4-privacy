Last updated: 27 March 2026

Chapter 4 ("we", "us", "our") operates the Chapter 4 application. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your information when you use our app and connected services.

When you connect your Instagram account through our app, we may access:

  • Your Instagram business profile information (name, username, profile picture, biography)

We use the information collected to:

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

4. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent and disconnect your Instagram account at any time

7. Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, email us at michael@chapter4.media. We will process your request within 30 days.
